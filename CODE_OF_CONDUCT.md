# Code of conduct

Science Around the Board is made by and for educators and students. We want everyone who takes part, from first-time question writers to experienced developers, to feel welcome and respected.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient. Many contributors are instructors, not software developers.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and explain the reasoning behind it.
- Welcome people of every background, identity, discipline and level of experience.
- Respect privacy. Never post student data, names or real class results in issues or pull requests.

These are not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexualised content, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and anything else that would be inappropriate in a classroom.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks it.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub, at the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (mark it as a conduct report), or by contacting [@hghezzi](https://github.com/hghezzi). Reports are handled confidentially.

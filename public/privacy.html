Privacy Notice

Science Around the Board · last updated October 2026

The short version

The game runs entirely in your web browser. There are no accounts and no game server. Nothing you type or answer in the game is sent to us. Your results reach your instructor only if you send them (see below).

What stays on your computer

  • The question files (.tsv or .lock) and any images you upload.
  • Team names, every answer, the pre- and post-game surveys and the in-game log.
  • The results file (CSV) you download. It is created on your computer and only goes where you choose to send it, for example your course page or your instructor's email.

Autosave

While a session is running, the game saves it in this browser only (its local storage), so it can be resumed after an accidental refresh. The save is deleted when you return to the main menu or exit the game, and it expires after 12 hours. It is never sent anywhere. Uploaded images are not saved.

Sending results to your instructor

Only when your instructor's question file includes a results link does the end screen offer Send results to instructor. Clicking it sends your team's answers, scores and the names or student IDs typed on that screen directly to a Google Sheet that belongs to your instructor; Google processes this data for your instructor. Nothing is sent unless you click Send, and it is never sent to the game's authors.

The Email results to instructor button only downloads the results file and opens your own email app with a draft. You choose whether to send it.

Optional analytics

If you click Allow analytics, the site loads Google Analytics to count anonymous visits: pages viewed, approximate location (country/region), browser and device type. This helps us understand how widely the game is used. We ask Google to anonymize IP addresses and we turn off advertising features. Analytics never receives question content, answers, survey responses, file contents or names.

If you click No thanks, Google Analytics is not loaded at all. The game works the same either way.

Your choice is saved in your browser. To change it, click Analytics settings at the bottom of the game's start page, or clear this site's data in your browser. Google's handling of analytics data is described in Google's Privacy Policy. Analytics data may be processed on servers outside your country.

For instructors

If your institution has rules about student data (for example, privacy impact assessments or data-residency requirements), you can ask students to choose No thanks. Game data leaves students' computers only when they click Send results (to your own Google Sheet) or send the results file themselves. If Google services aren't allowed for student data, use the email option or a course-page upload instead, and ask students to type student numbers or initials rather than full names if your rules require it.
